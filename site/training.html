How it works

Built around you

Every PT journey at KROSS PARK starts with an assessment. Your coach maps where you are, where you want to be, and builds the program that gets you there.

Sessions run 1:1 or as a duo in our indoor studio, built for focused work, with the rooftop strength park and ice bath available when your program calls for them. Strength, fat loss, HYROX prep, return from injury, sport specific work. Whatever the goal, it gets a plan.
