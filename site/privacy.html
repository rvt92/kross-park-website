policy

What we collect on krosspark.club, why, and how to reach us about it. Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

KROSS PARK, 5/9 Soi Phrom Si 1, Khlong Tan Nuea, Watthana, Bangkok 10110, Thailand. Email krosspark2026@gmail.com.

Enquiry form

When you send the form on our contact page we receive your name, email, phone number if you give it, what you are interested in and your message. The form is delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which passes it to our inbox. We use it only to answer you. We do not sell or share it.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the site is used, and Google Ads to measure whether our ads lead to a visit, a message or an app download. Both set cookies and record your approximate location, device and pages viewed. Google's own privacy policy applies to that data. You can block these cookies in your browser or with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Third parties on the page

The map on the contact page is embedded from Google Maps. Links to the KROSS App, LINE, WhatsApp and Instagram take you to those services, each with its own privacy policy. Bookings and payments happen in the KROSS App, not on this site.

Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email krosspark2026@gmail.com and we handle it.
