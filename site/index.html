The Park

One rooftop.
Everything you need.

KROSS PARK is the newest space from the team behind KROSS, Thailand's largest padel chain. We looked at a rooftop in Phrom Phong and saw what nobody else did: a training ground in the sky. Now the morning starts with pickleball before the heat, sleds run against the skyline, barbells move in the open air, and the day ends with a cold plunge and a view.

Come for a game, a class, a session with your coach, or all three. The skyline comes free.
